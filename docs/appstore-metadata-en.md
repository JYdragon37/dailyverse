## Description (4,000 characters max)

```
Wake up to God's Word, not just an alarm.

morning manna is a Christian alarm app that replaces the jarring sound of a regular alarm with a Bible verse — so your quiet time starts the moment you open your eyes, no extra step required.

───────────────────────────────────────
✦ Key Features
───────────────────────────────────────

📖 Verse Alarm
When your alarm goes off, today's Bible verse appears right alongside it. See it instantly on your lock screen, then open the app for the full interpretation and a practical way to apply it today.

⏰ Smart Alarms
Set up to 3 alarms. Choose the verse theme that fits what you need — hope, courage, gratitude, peace, and more.

🌤 Weather-Aware Verses
Each verse comes with a mood-matched background image reflecting today's weather and temperature.

♥ Save Your Favorites
Save the verses that speak to you, along with a snapshot of the date and weather when you saved them.

🌙 Three Modes Throughout the Day
Morning (5am–12pm), Afternoon (12pm–8pm), and Evening (8pm–5am) — the verse and background automatically shift to match the time of day.

───────────────────────────────────────
✦ Scripture Source
───────────────────────────────────────

King James Version (public domain)

───────────────────────────────────────
✦ Who It's For
───────────────────────────────────────

• You want a daily quiet time but keep falling off after a few days
• Your morning alarm feels cold and mechanical
• You want to hold onto a verse even on your busiest days
• You want your faith habits to feel natural, not forced
```
